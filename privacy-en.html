Privacy Policy

Co-op Metronome (みんなでメトロノーム) for iPhone / Apple Watch / Android / Wear OS
Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Co-op Metronome ("the App") is a metronome that lets people in different places feel the same tempo together. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

The App needs no account and no sign-in. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising ID.

2. Information we do not collect

The App never collects the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendars, photos, or files
  • Advertising ID or any other identifier used for advertising
  • Audio (the App does not use the microphone)
  • Usage analytics or activity history

The App contains no ads and no in-app purchases.

3. Information sent when you connect with others

When you play together in a room over the internet, the App keeps everyone on the same tempo. It sends only what is needed to play together.

Rooms (over the internet)

Using a Room Key (4 digits), the App exchanges the following through the external service Ably:

  • The Room Key (4 digits, created by your device or entered by you)
  • Tempo (BPM), the number of beats per bar, and which beats are accented
  • The time playback starts
  • Whether you are the host or a participant
  • A temporary identifier (a random string created again each time the App starts; it is not linked to you)

A Room Key is shared only when you tell it to someone. Only people who enter the same Room Key can join the same room. A Room Key cannot be used to identify you.

4. Bug reports

Information needed to investigate problems is sent only when you tap "?" (Contact) at the top right of the screen. Nothing is sent unless you tap it. When you tap it, the App shows what will be sent, and you can choose whether to send it.

The following information is sent:

  • Your platform (android / ios)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Device language setting
  • Connection state at that moment (room / not connected), Room Key, and number of people
  • Whether a smartwatch is connected
  • Whether the metronome was playing at that moment
  • Date and time of the report

It does not include your name, email address, or location. The report is recorded in a Google Sheets spreadsheet through a form on the external service Tally, and is used only to investigate problems.

About the notification (Android / Wear OS)
While you are connected to a room, a notification appears saying that you are connected to a room. Android requires it so the room connection is not cut when you turn off the screen or open another app. The notification is just this one line and does not send anything. It disappears when you finish playing and leave the room.
How the App keeps running on Apple Watch
On Apple Watch, the App uses watchOS extended runtime (mindfulness) so the tempo does not stop when you lower your wrist.
About the click sound
The App delivers the tempo as vibration. You can turn the click sound on or off in the App (smartphone only, off by default). Smartwatches use vibration only. The sound is generated on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

6. External services

The App uses the following external services:

  • Ably (Ably Real-time Ltd.) — relays room communication. Due to the nature of the connection, information such as your IP address may be logged by Ably.
  • Tally / Google Sheets — receive and record bug reports.
  • Google Play (Android / Wear OS) — distributes the App. Google provides statistics such as install counts, which do not include personally identifiable information.
  • App Store / TestFlight (iPhone / Apple Watch) — distribute the App. Apple provides statistics such as install counts, which do not include personally identifiable information.

The App never sells or shares your information with third parties.

7. Data storage

  • Settings such as tempo and beats per bar are stored on your device. If device backup is turned on (such as Google backup on Android or iCloud backup on iPhone), they may be included in the backup.
  • Information exchanged in a room is used only at that moment to keep everyone in time.
  • Bug reports are kept in the spreadsheet for 90 days after they are sent, and then deleted. To have a report deleted sooner, see "8. Request data deletion" below.

8. Request data deletion

Co-op Metronome (developer: HANAASA) accepts requests to delete submitted bug reports at any time.

Data that can be deleted

The content sent from "?" (Contact) at the top right of the App screen. Specifically, the following 11 items:

  • Platform (android / ios), device model, OS version
  • App version, device language setting
  • Connection state at that moment, Room Key, number of people
  • Smartwatch connection state
  • Whether the metronome was playing at that moment
  • Date and time sent

The App stores no other user data. Settings such as tempo and beats per bar are stored on your device, so they are removed from your device when you uninstall the App (no request needed). Settings included in a device backup can be managed in your backup settings (Google account or iCloud).

Retention period

Bug reports are kept for 90 days after they are sent, and then deleted.

How to request deletion

Please email us at the address below and tell us you would like your data deleted.

minnademetronome.support@gmail.com

To help us find your report, please include:

  • The approximate date and time you sent the bug report
  • Your device model (for example, Pixel 8)

Because there are no accounts, we use these two details to find the record. We will delete it and let you know by email when it is done.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.
